01 · About

Who I am

I’m Raphaël Catenacci, an engineering student at ENSAE Paris, part of the Institut Polytechnique de Paris, graduating in 2027. I am currently completing the final year of a Master’s degree in Financial Engineering at the Université de Montréal in Canada.

I am passionate about quantitative finance, machine learning, and software development. I build data-driven financial tools, from automated market-data pipelines and portfolio-risk models to pricing engines and machine-learning research workflows.

Before moving into finance and technology, I competed as a high-level BMX Racing athlete, reaching 9th in the U18 UCI World Ranking in 2019. This experience shaped the discipline, resilience, and performance mindset that I bring to every technical and quantitative project.
